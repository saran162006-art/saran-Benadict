Key Insights

  • The dataset was cleaned from 310 raw records to 300 unique, consistent records — 10 exact duplicates were removed.
  • Missing values in Rating, Votes, Budget, Revenue and Duration were filled using median imputation; missing Director/Country were filled with mode/"Unknown".
  • Outliers in Duration, Budget, Revenue and Votes were capped using the IQR (1.5x) method rather than dropped, preserving all 300 records.
  • Genre and Country text values were standardized (e.g. "sci-fi", "Sci Fi" → "Sci-Fi"; "usa", "U.S.A" → "USA").
  • Sci-Fi is the most frequently produced genre, while Thriller movies have the highest average rating (6.90).
  • Budget and Revenue show a strong positive correlation (0.75) — higher-budget films tend to earn more.
  • The most profitable movie in the dataset is "Shadow of Warrior Returns" with a profit of $276.7M.
